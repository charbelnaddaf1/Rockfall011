RockFall privacy policy

What the game stores

Your progress (wave, coins, gems, upgrades, cannons, settings) is saved on your device. The game has no account of its own and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your scores (highest wave and Endless scores) to Google's leaderboards. Other players can see your Play Games name and scores there, depending on your Play Games profile settings. To show the leaderboards, the game reads your Play Games player ID and display name from Google; it does not store them or send them anywhere else. You can play without signing in.

Ads, measurement and crash reports

The game shows ads through Yodo1 MAS, an ad mediation service run by Yodo1. MAS chooses each ad from one of these ad networks: Google AdMob, AppLovin, BidMachine, Bigo Ads, Meta Audience Network, Digital Turbine (Fyber), InMobi, ironSource, Mintegral, Moloco, Pangle, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle) and Yodo1's own network. The game also measures how it is used through Google Firebase Analytics, and sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics.

These services may collect your device's advertising ID, an app instance ID, device and app information (such as phone model, Android version and app version), approximate location from your IP address, how you use the game and how you interact with ads, and details of crashes and errors. They use it to show and measure ads, to prevent ad fraud, to understand which parts of the game work, and to fix problems. Yodo1 and the ad networks may share this information with each other to choose and measure ads. The game itself never sends your name, email or Play Games account to these services, and does not ask for your precise location.

Your choices

On first launch the game asks your age and, where the law requires it, for your consent to personalised ads and to the sale or sharing of your data. Analytics and crash reports are turned on only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the game's settings (Settings, then Privacy & ad consent). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, then Ads).

See Yodo1's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Each ad network named above has its own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, Google Play handles the payment. The game never sees your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If a player tells the game they are under 13, ads are limited to those suitable for children and are not personalised, and analytics and crash reports stay off.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset progress" in the game's settings, or uninstall the game, to erase everything stored on your device. Scores on Google Play Games leaderboards are managed by Google: you can hide or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings.
