Rockfall privacy policy

What the game stores

Your progress (wave, coins, gems, upgrades, cannons, settings) is saved on your device only. The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Ads and measurement

The released game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures anonymous usage through Google Firebase. These services may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information, and how you use the game, to show and measure ads and to help us improve the game. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time in the game settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The game never sees your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset progress" in the game settings, or uninstall the game, to erase everything stored on your device.
